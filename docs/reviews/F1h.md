## Focus answers

1. **Can a cue still delete a date? Not in the normal case.** The second net (`Moodle.swift:630-631`) clears `cueEnd` when no date is found at/after it; the date loop then keeps every date and `moved` stays nil. “The quiz on 20 Oct has moved to room 3” now keeps 20 Oct with `moved == nil`, and the bare-number/floor/week cases do not match the new date-shape lookahead. The only deletion I can still construct is with an **invalid post-cue date** (for example “moved to 31 Feb”): the second net sees day 31 at/after the cue, keeps `cueEnd`, the calendar constructor rejects the date, and the pre-cue valid date is dropped, leaving no event. That is an edge, not the room-number class.
2. **Relative-word position — fixed in one direction, still not cue-aware.** `at` now points at the word actually selected (`:621`), which makes “move tomorrow's office hours to today at 3 pm” resolve to today (test passes). But selection is still **today before tomorrow, and the first weekday**, and relative parsing runs only when no absolute date exists (`:605-606`). Therefore:
   - “move today's office hours to tomorrow” selects **today** (before the cue), the second net sees no date after it, and the event stays on the old day with `moved == nil`.
   - “Quiz on Tuesday moved to Thursday” selects Tuesday (the first weekday), not Thursday.
   - “Quiz on 20 Oct moved to Thursday” finds the absolute 20 Oct, skips relative parsing entirely, sees no date after the cue, and keeps 20 Oct.
   These do not lose the card, but they show the wrong day and lose the move. The test covers only the “tomorrow → today” direction and absolute new dates.
3. **Range detection — correct for the page/PDF `onDays` path, but `namedDays` applies ranges to every source.** `onDays` restricts to `.coursePage`/`.pdf`, requires `Moodle.range(title)` and a ≤7-day span (`Model.swift:608-618`); the tested forms (“1-2 Dec”, “Dec 1-2”, “1 Oct – 15 Dec” no range) behave. However `Moodle.namedDays` calls `range(text)` unconditionally (`Moodle.swift:540`), so posts and mail with “1 Dec to 3 Dec” (often a **move**) get both days in their day set, and a long “1 Oct – 15 Dec” range adds both endpoints even though `onDays` rejects the span. This contradicts §2.2 rule 6 (“posts and mail never get ranges”, “spanning ≤7 days”) and can change the day-set guard’s decisions.
4. **PDF-skip mark guard — new marks work, old board-state marks do not.** `Mark.pdf` is an optional field (`BoardState.swift:12`); a new mark on a cluster with a PDF gets `pdf = true` (`Store.swift:89`) and `pruneMarks(..., keepPDF: true)` keeps it, which the new test checks. But a `Mark` written before F1h decodes with **`pdf == nil`**, and the guard is `keepPDF && $0.value.pdf == true` (`BoardState.swift:52`). So an existing PDF-date mark older than 30 days is still pruned in a run where its PDF is unreadable/missing — exactly the migration case in the focus. There is no load-time migration or conservative `nil` handling, and the test uses only `pdf: true`.

## Findings

1. **High (conditional) — old board-state PDF marks are not protected by the PDF-skip guard.** `BoardState.swift:12` + `:50-53`, `Store.swift:89`, `:97`. A mark from B5/B6/F1g has no `pdf` key, decodes nil, and fails `pdf == true`; if its item date is more than 30 days old and a PDF gave no text this run, `pruneMarks` drops it. The item later returns unmarked. Fix: treat `pdf == nil` conservatively when `keepPDF` is set (e.g. `pdf != false`), or migrate old marks on load (they predate the flag, so keeping them while a PDF is unreadable is the safe default), and add an old-JSON fixture test.
2. **Medium — relative date selection is not cue-aware and mixed absolute+relative is skipped.** `Moodle.swift:605-621`: the selected word is today before tomorrow and the first weekday; relative parsing only when `found.isEmpty`. Scenarios: “move today's office hours to tomorrow” → today; “Quiz on Tuesday moved to Thursday” → Tuesday; “Quiz on 20 Oct moved to Thursday” → 20 Oct, because the Thursday is never added to `found` and the second net then ignores the cue. The event is not deleted but the wrong date and `moved == nil` are shown. Fix: collect every relative word (with its position), or after a cue parse the relative word after `cueEnd`; parse relative words even when absolute dates exist.
3. **Medium/Low — `namedDays` ranges are not limited to page/PDF or to ≤7 days.** `Moodle.swift:540` adds `range(text)` endpoints for every source and every span; the `onDays` restriction at `Model.swift:614` does not apply to the `days` sets used by the F1e weekday guard. A news/mail post with a “from X to Y” move or a “1 Oct – 15 Dec” phrase therefore gets extra days; this violates F1h #3 and can let the day-set guard make a different merge decision. Restrict the range union to `.coursePage`/`.pdf` and apply the same ≤7-day check, or keep ranges only in `onDays`.
4. **Low — the second net can still lose the only date when the post-cue date is invalid.** `Moodle.swift:630` accepts any day 1…31 after the cue; the loop at `:621` later rejects an impossible date, but the pre-cue valid date has already been filtered. “Quiz on 20 Oct moved to 31 Feb” produces no event. Make the second net check that the post-cue date actually constructs a valid `Date` (and, if none does, clear `cueEnd`).
5. **Low — range form and year-crossing coverage.** The tests cover `1-2 Dec`, `Dec 1-2` and the long `1 Oct – 15 Dec`; they do not cover “1 Dec – 3 Dec”, “Dec 1 - Dec 3”, “1 Dec to/until/till/through 3 Dec”, or “Dec 1-2”. `onDays` maps each endpoint to the nearest year independently, so a genuinely year-crossing range (31 Dec – 2 Jan) can produce `lo > hi` and be ignored. Add these forms and a January/December case.
6. **Low — range items add their own date even when it is outside the named range.** `Model.swift:614-618` starts `out = [own]` and adds every day between the endpoints; a page/PDF item whose own date is outside the range still counts as “same day” on its own date. Use `own` only when it lies in the range (or leave it in for the equal-date case explicitly).
